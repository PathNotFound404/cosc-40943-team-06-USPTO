First release of USPTO Patent Intelligence for macOS (Apple silicon, macOS 15 Sequoia or later).

**What it does**

- Imports weekly office action bulk files, rejecting bad records with a reason (`UC-FEAT-decompile-files`)
- Builds the staged dataset after each import (`UC-FEAT-clean-data`)
- Creates visualizations of USPTO examination activity (`UC-FEAT-create-visuals`)
- Shows the source records behind any visualization or bar (`UC-FEAT-verify-source`)
- Exports labeled PNG images for posts (`UC-FEAT-export-visuals`)

Everything runs on your Mac. Nothing is sent over the internet.

**Install**

Download `USPTO-Patent-Intelligence-1.0.0-arm64.dmg` below, open it, and drag the app into Applications. The app is not registered with Apple, so the first launch needs one confirmation in System Settings → Privacy & Security → **Open Anyway**. The [user guide](https://github.com/PathNotFound404/cosc-40943-team-06-USPTO/blob/main/docs/user-guide.md) walks through it.
